    Choosing a method#

    What is your output? A number: regression. A category: classification. None: PCA or clustering

    What are your inputs? A table of features: linear/logistic, lasso, random forest, boosting. Images: CNN. Sequences: AR model, RNN

    Always: 1. start with a simple baseline; 2. compare methods by cross-validation; 3. test once, at the end, on data never used; 4. check that the result makes physical sense

    The four rules every group should follow in the project.

    Group project presentations#

    10 minutes per group, then 5 minutes of questions

    1. The problem, and why it matters. 2. The data: source, size, inputs, output. 3. The methods you compared, with a table of 10-fold CV errors. 4. Your best model: what it tells you about the problem. 5. Limitations, and what you would do next

    Every member presents part of the talk and can answer questions on any part of the work

    Submit on Google Classroom the slides and the Colab notebook, which must run from top to bottom

    Summary#

    1. Unsupervised learning finds structure without an output to predict
    1. PCA summarises many correlated variables with a few components; the scree plot shows how many
    1. PCA reconstruction error detects readings that break the normal pattern
    1. \(k\)-means and hierarchical clustering find groups; scaling decides what “similar” means
    1. Clusters are a starting point: check that they are stable and meaningful
